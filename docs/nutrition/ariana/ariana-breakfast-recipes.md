# The Ariana Program — Breakfasts

*Whole-food porridge & overnight-oat bowls for FatLoss Coach / HUMANS. Draft content — PM FatLoss, for CEO review.*

The Ariana Program is a plant-forward breakfast collection built on one simple base — rolled oats, a milk of your choice (or water), and real fruit — then finished with seeds, nuts, and warming spices. Every bowl here is a variation on the same wholesome idea: slow-release oats for steady morning energy, fruit for natural sweetness and fibre, and a handful of seeds or nuts for healthy fats and a little staying power. The philosophy is **build-your-own**: pick the base, pick the fruit, pick the toppings. Use high-quality, organic ingredients where you can — the fewer and better, the better the bowl.

These are breakfasts to feel good about and to look forward to. They are food, not medicine: the nutrition figures are a guide for your day, never a prescription.

**How to read the nutrition:** values are per serving and are estimates calculated from standard food databases (USDA-class reference values). They are meant to be realistic and consistent across the set — not lab-measured. Actual values shift with your exact brands, portions, and how ripe the banana is. Where a quantity is an estimate, it is noted on the recipe. Toppings marked "to taste" are not always counted in the headline numbers; the assumption line tells you what was.

**A note on the base:** unless stated, each recipe assumes **50 g rolled oats** and **200 ml semi-skimmed milk** (or an unsweetened plant milk / water — which lowers the calories and protein a little). Swap freely; the bowl survives it.

## 1. Golden Turmeric Porridge

*Photo: `ariana-01.jpg`* · Serves 1 · Prep 5 min · Cook 6 min · **Hot porridge**

A sunny, gently spiced bowl — turmeric and mashed banana stirred through creamy oats, crowned with fresh blueberries and a scatter of pecans and walnuts.

**Ingredients**
- 50 g rolled oats
- 200 ml milk of choice (or water)
- 1/2 ripe banana (~60 g), mashed into the porridge
- 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
- pinch of black pepper (helps the turmeric; optional)
- 60 g fresh blueberries
- 15 g pecans (about 4 halves)
- 10 g walnuts
- drizzle of honey or maple — to taste, optional

**Method**
1. Simmer oats and milk 5–6 min, stirring, until creamy.
2. Stir in the mashed banana, turmeric, and a pinch of black pepper.
3. Spoon into a bowl. Top with blueberries, pecans, and walnuts.
4. Finish with an optional drizzle of honey.

**Nutrition per serving** — **~455 kcal** · Protein 14 g · Carbs 55 g · Fat 20 g · Fibre 9 g · Sugar 20 g
*Assumes semi-skimmed milk; honey not counted (adds ~20 kcal/tsp).*

*Why it's good:* oats and banana give steady, slow-release energy, while pecans and walnuts add wholesome plant fats — a warm, satisfying start to the day.

## 2. Blood-Orange & Apple Bircher

*Photo: `ariana-02.jpg`* · Serves 1 · Prep 10 min + overnight soak · **Overnight / Bircher**

A fresh, Swiss-style Bircher: oats soaked soft overnight, folded with grated apple and banana, brightened with juicy red-orange and a spoon of flax.

**Ingredients**
- 50 g rolled oats
- 150 ml milk of choice
- 60 g plain yoghurt (dairy or plant)
- 1 small apple (~100 g), grated
- 1/2 banana (~60 g), sliced
- 1/2 blood/red orange (~75 g), segmented
- 1 tbsp ground flax seeds (~10 g)

**Method**
1. The night before, mix oats, milk, yoghurt, and grated apple. Cover and chill overnight.
2. In the morning, loosen with a splash of milk if needed.
3. Top with banana slices, orange segments, and a sprinkle of flax.

**Nutrition per serving** — **~420 kcal** · Protein 15 g · Carbs 66 g · Fat 11 g · Fibre 11 g · Sugar 30 g
*Assumes semi-skimmed milk + low-fat yoghurt. Overnight soak, no cooking.*

*Why it's good:* grated apple and flax bring plenty of fibre, and the Bircher soak makes the oats easy and gentle — a cool, refreshing bowl for warmer mornings.

## 3. Banana-Apple Porridge with Chia & Sunflower

*Photo: `ariana-03.jpg`* · Serves 1 · Prep 5 min · Cook 6 min · **Hot porridge**

Creamy banana-apple oats topped with a generous spoon of chia and sunflower seeds, framed by crisp green apple and sweet red grapes.

**Ingredients**
- 50 g rolled oats
- 200 ml milk of choice
- 1/2 banana (~60 g), mashed in
- 1/2 apple (~75 g) grated into the porridge, plus a few slices to serve
- 1 tbsp chia seeds (~12 g)
- 1 tbsp sunflower seeds (~10 g)
- 60 g red grapes

**Method**
1. Cook oats and milk 5–6 min until creamy; stir in the mashed banana and grated apple.
2. Spoon into a bowl.
3. Top with a line of chia and sunflower seeds, apple slices, and grapes.

**Nutrition per serving** — **~470 kcal** · Protein 15 g · Carbs 66 g · Fat 16 g · Fibre 12 g · Sugar 28 g
*Assumes semi-skimmed milk.*

*Why it's good:* chia and sunflower seeds add fibre and healthy fats, and the double hit of apple and grape keeps it naturally sweet without anything added.

## 4. Flax & Chia Overnight Porridge with Summer Fruits

*Photo: `ariana-04.jpg`* · Serves 1 · Prep 8 min + overnight soak · **Overnight oats**

Silky overnight oats thickened with flax and chia, piled with fresh peach (or nectarine) and ripe strawberry.

**Ingredients**
- 50 g rolled oats
- 220 ml milk of choice
- 1 tbsp chia seeds (~12 g)
- 1 tbsp ground flax seeds (~10 g)
- 1/2 banana (~60 g), mashed in (optional, for sweetness)
- 1 peach or nectarine (~120 g), diced
- 4 strawberries (~60 g), sliced

**Method**
1. The night before, stir oats, milk, chia, flax, and mashed banana together. Cover and chill.
2. In the morning, stir — it will be thick and pudding-like; loosen with milk if you like.
3. Top with diced peach and sliced strawberry.

**Nutrition per serving** — **~440 kcal** · Protein 16 g · Carbs 58 g · Fat 16 g · Fibre 14 g · Sugar 24 g
*Assumes semi-skimmed milk. Overnight soak, no cooking.*

*Why it's good:* the flax-and-chia combo makes this one of the highest-fibre bowls in the set, and the overnight soak means a two-minute, spoon-and-go morning.

## 5. Build-Your-Own Porridge (Pistachio & Kiwi)

*Photo: `ariana-05.jpg`* · Serves 1 · Prep 5 min · Cook 6 min · **Hot porridge**

The signature Ariana bowl — your base, your fruit, your toppings. This version: creamy oats with pistachios and pecans, fresh kiwi, blueberries, and banana.

**Ingredients**
- 50 g rolled oats
- 200 ml milk of choice
- 1/2 banana (~60 g), sliced or mashed
- 15 g pistachios (shelled)
- 10 g pecans
- 1 kiwi (~75 g), sliced
- 40 g blueberries

**Method**
1. Cook oats and milk 5–6 min until creamy; stir in banana.
2. Spoon into a bowl.
3. Top with pistachios, pecans, kiwi, and blueberries — arrange however you like.

**Nutrition per serving** — **~460 kcal** · Protein 15 g · Carbs 58 g · Fat 19 g · Fibre 9 g · Sugar 20 g
*Assumes semi-skimmed milk. Numbers shift with your chosen toppings — this is the template bowl.*

*Why it's good:* this is the "make it yours" bowl — swap the fruit and nuts to whatever's fresh and you keep the same wholesome, balanced shape every time.

## 6. Banana-Cinnamon Porridge with Fresh Berries

*Photo: `ariana-06.jpg`* · Serves 1 · Prep 5 min · Cook 6 min · **Hot porridge**

Warm cinnamon-banana oats under a crown of raspberries, red grapes, and pecans, finished with a dusting of cinnamon.

**Ingredients**
- 50 g rolled oats
- 200 ml milk of choice
- 1 small banana (~90 g), half mashed in + half sliced on top
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 40 g raspberries
- 50 g red grapes
- 10 g pecans

**Method**
1. Cook oats and milk 5–6 min; stir in mashed banana and most of the cinnamon.
2. Spoon into a bowl; lay banana slices, raspberries, grapes, and pecans on top.
3. Dust with the remaining cinnamon.

**Nutrition per serving** — **~430 kcal** · Protein 13 g · Carbs 68 g · Fat 13 g · Fibre 10 g · Sugar 30 g
*Assumes semi-skimmed milk.*

*Why it's good:* warming cinnamon and sweet banana make this a comforting bowl, and the fresh berries add fibre and a bright, tart finish.

## 7. Forest-Fruits Porridge with Almond Butter & Nuts

*Photo: `ariana-07.jpg`* · Serves 1 · Prep 5 min · Cook 6 min · **Hot porridge**

A rich berry-purple bowl — oats cooked with mixed forest fruits, swirled with almond butter and topped with walnuts, almonds, and pecans.

**Ingredients**
- 50 g rolled oats
- 200 ml milk of choice
- 80 g mixed frozen forest fruits (blueberry, blackberry, raspberry, cherry), stirred in while cooking
- 1 tbsp almond butter (~16 g)
- 10 g walnuts
- 8 g almonds
- 8 g pecans
- pinch of cinnamon — optional

**Method**
1. Cook oats and milk 5–6 min, adding the forest fruits for the last 2 min so they soften and colour the porridge.
2. Spoon into a bowl; swirl the almond butter on top.
3. Scatter walnuts, almonds, and pecans; add a pinch of cinnamon.

**Nutrition per serving** — **~515 kcal** · Protein 17 g · Carbs 52 g · Fat 27 g · Fibre 11 g · Sugar 17 g
*Assumes semi-skimmed milk. The richest bowl in the set — almond butter + three nuts.*

*Why it's good:* almond butter and a trio of nuts make this a hearty, higher-fat bowl that keeps you full — ideal before a busy or active morning.

## 8. Rose & Berry Forest Porridge

*Photo: `ariana-08.jpg`* · Serves 1 · Prep 5 min · Cook 6 min · **Hot porridge**

A delicately rose-scented berry porridge — oats cooked with forest fruits and a whisper of rose water, finished simply with walnuts and pecans. Best with high-quality, organic ingredients.

**Ingredients**
- 50 g rolled oats
- 200 ml milk of choice
- 80 g mixed forest fruits (blueberry, cherry, raspberry), stirred in
- 1/4 tsp rose water (to taste — start small)
- 1/2 banana (~60 g), mashed in
- 12 g walnuts
- 8 g pecans

**Method**
1. Cook oats and milk 5–6 min; add forest fruits for the last 2 min, then the mashed banana.
2. Stir in the rose water a few drops at a time, tasting as you go.
3. Spoon into a bowl and top with walnuts and pecans.

**Nutrition per serving** — **~430 kcal** · Protein 14 g · Carbs 56 g · Fat 17 g · Fibre 10 g · Sugar 20 g
*Assumes semi-skimmed milk; rose water is negligible calories.*

*Why it's good:* the rose water turns a simple berry bowl into something special with nothing added but fragrance — a little everyday luxury.

## 9. Pan-Cooked Banana Porridge with Fresh Berries

*Photo: `ariana-09.jpg`* · Serves 1 · Prep 5 min · Cook 7 min · **Hot porridge (one pan)**

The quick, no-fuss classic — oats and mashed banana cooked together in the pan until creamy, then folded through with fresh strawberries and blueberries right before serving.

**Ingredients**
- 50 g rolled oats
- 220 ml milk of choice (or water)
- 1 ripe banana (~100 g), mashed
- 5 strawberries (~75 g), diced
- 50 g blueberries
- pinch of cinnamon — optional

**Method**
1. Cook oats, milk, and mashed banana together 6–7 min, stirring, until thick and creamy.
2. Off the heat, fold in most of the strawberries and blueberries.
3. Scatter the rest on top; add a pinch of cinnamon.

**Nutrition per serving** — **~395 kcal** · Protein 13 g · Carbs 72 g · Fat 7 g · Fibre 9 g · Sugar 30 g
*Assumes semi-skimmed milk. The lightest bowl in the set — no added nuts or seeds.*

*Why it's good:* ripe banana does the sweetening, so there's nothing added — a simple, lower-fat bowl that comes together in one pan.

## Honest note on the numbers

Your actual bowl will vary with the exact brands you buy, how much you scoop, the ripeness of the fruit, and your choice of milk — switching from semi-skimmed dairy milk to water or an unsweetened plant milk, for example, will lower the calories and protein a little.
